# Scoundrel

Scoundrel is a single player rogue-like card game by Zach Gage and Kurt Bieg.

You can find the rules [here](http://stfj.net/art/2011/Scoundrel.pdf)

To play this version of the game, just press the draw pile or the open cards.
There is one hidden feature. To fight a monster with your bare hands (skipping
the weapon), press the controll key on the left side of your keyboard.
